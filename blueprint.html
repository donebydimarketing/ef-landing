The workbook

The Delegation Blueprint

Eight pages that tell you what to hand off first, what to keep, and the order to do it in.

What it is

A workbook you fill in once. It takes about forty minutes.

At the end you have three tasks picked, written down, and ready to give to somebody else. You also have a way to check in three weeks whether it worked.

What is inside

  1. What this is, and how to use it.
  2. Your business snapshot. Where your time actually goes, across six areas.
  3. Choosing your first three, using a keep or leave rule.
  4. Your delegation plan, part one.
  5. Your delegation plan, part two.
  6. Who does it.
  7. The measurement sheet. Today in one column, three weeks from now in the other.
  8. After three weeks. What worked, what half worked, what did not.

The six areas

Inbox and messages Calendar and scheduling Money in and out Content and posting Following through with people Files, systems and admin

Every task you do sits in one of these. That is why the order matters.

This is for you if

  • You spend hours on work somebody else could do.
  • You tried getting help before and it did not stick.
  • You know you need help and you cannot say what to hand over first.
  • You are a long way from hiring someone full time.

Skip it if

  • You already have a team running well without you.
  • You want somebody else to do the deciding for you.
  • You want people doing the work with you starting now. That is the guided version below.

What happens in three weeks

You come back to page seven and fill in the second column.

Your own hours sit side by side. You see what moved and what did not, in your own handwriting, on one page. That is the whole point of the sheet.

What waiting costs

Every week you hold on to the small tasks, you pay twice. Once in the hours. Once in the work you never got to because the hours were gone.

The tasks do not get easier to explain later. They get bigger and more tangled, and by then they are in your head only.
