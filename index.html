What people keep telling us

I need help. But I am not ready to hire.

If you have said that out loud, or thought it at 11pm with the laptop still open, you are in the right place.

The spot you are in

You are past doing all of it yourself. You are not at job posts, interviews, payroll, and hoping you picked the right person.

Most people sit in that gap for months. They keep the whole business in their head and call it being busy.

There is a middle ground.

You do not hire anyone. You hand off a few specific things and you keep the rest.

No job post. No interviews. No committing to a full role before you know what you would even give them.

Start with the blueprint

The Delegation Blueprint is a short form. About five minutes.

It asks what your days actually look like and what keeps getting pushed to tomorrow. You get back a plain list: what to hand off first, what to keep, and what order to do it in.

No call to book. Nothing to sit through.

What usually comes off the plate first

  • The inbox you open, look at, and close again
  • The follow ups you meant to send last week
  • Scheduling, rescheduling, reminders
  • Posting the thing you already wrote
  • The invoice you have to send before you get paid
  • The small task that has been on the list since June

This is for you if

  • You are the bottleneck and you know it
  • You are good at the actual work and stuck on everything around it
  • You have tried apps, timers and lists, and the problem was never the app

Not for you if

  • You want someone to run the business for you
  • You are ready to hire a full time person today

Fill in the blueprint.

Five minutes. Then you will know what to hand off first.
