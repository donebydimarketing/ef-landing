How to use it

  1. Fill in the business snapshot first. Six categories, and you are only marking where things actually sit today.
  2. Set one goal per category with a number you can measure against. Rough is fine. A number you can check in three weeks beats a perfect one you cannot.
  3. Work through the categories in the order given. That order is the whole point of the document.
  4. Come back to the measurement sheet in three weeks and fill in the second column.

One thing before you start

Do not begin with the task that annoys you most. Begin with the smallest one you keep pushing to tomorrow. It is the one quietly costing you the week, and it is the easiest to hand to somebody else.
